💡 What did we just learn?

Data: Webcam frames and student-provided labels form the training set.

Algorithms: A pretrained neural network converts images into numerical features; a nearest-neighbour algorithm compares new examples with training examples.

Machine learning: We reuse learned visual features and learn the three gesture classes from your examples.

Evaluation: New hands, different lighting or unusual angles can reveal mistakes. Add more varied training data and test again.

Privacy: webcam frames and image features stay in your browser; nothing is uploaded to an application server. The pretrained model downloads from a public CDN. Camera permission and an internet connection are required. Run on localhost or HTTPS.
